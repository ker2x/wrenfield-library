# The Wrenfield Library — Licence of Circulation

### An instrument of the Registry, granted at the issue of 1504, and not catalogued with the holdings

- **Instrument:** the Registry's own series, uncatalogued; not a volume, not a holding, and not counted
  among the forty-nine.
- **Grantor:** the Wrenfield Library of the University of Alderwick, for the shelf as issued in 1504.
- **Grantee:** every reader, whether entered in the Register of Readers or not. No fee is levied on this
  instrument, and no form is required of the reader.
- **Term:** perpetual, irrevocable, and not withdrawn where a volume has been kept past the quarter-day.
- **Copies:** as many as thou wilt take. None is to be returned, and none is to be asked after.
- **Where it is posted:** in each of the three reading rooms. A fourth copy was struck in 1487 for a
  fourth room, and is kept at the desk, the room not having received it.

Be it known to all who take up a volume of this shelf — whether they read it, or copy it, or cut it
about, or set it to music, or put it in a cupboard — that the terms upon which it is given are these and
no others; and that they are drawn in the register of the old line, in which the Registry keeps its
instruments, and which the Registry has been asked to modernise four times — in 1431, in 1466, in 1491
and in 1503 — and has declined four times, the meaning of the words being the whole of the grant.

1. **Of copying.** Thou shalt copy, in whole or in part, by any hand and by any means, and shalt not be
   hindered in the copying; nor shall any fee be asked of thee for the taking of a copy, nor any form
   required of thee before it is taken.

2. **Of the naming.** Thou shalt name the Library, and the hand that wrote, and the call number under
   which the volume stands; and where thou hast altered the work, thou shalt say plainly that thou hast
   altered it, and shalt not father thy alterations upon the Library, nor upon the hand that wrote.

3. **Of further conditions.** Thou shalt not add terms of thine own, nor wrap the work about with any
   further condition, nor shut by a side door what this instrument leaves open by the front. The grant
   is given whole, or it is not given.

4. **Of what thou passest on.** Thou shalt set thy copies, and whatsoever thou makest out of the work,
   under this same licence, that what is given freely stays free in thy hands as in ours.

5. **Of the Library's name.** Thou shalt not say, nor leave it to be supposed, that the Library has
   endorsed thee, thy use, or thy work, nor that thou speakest with the Registry's warrant. The Library
   has endorsed nothing since 1147 and does not propose to begin.

6. **Of honour.** The author's honour is not the Registry's to grant away. It remains with the hand that
   wrote; this instrument neither takes it nor confers it.

7. **Of the words.** Thou shalt not shorten this instrument for a poster, nor paraphrase it for a
   colophon, nor keep the sense of it and let the words go. A paraphrase is not the licence: the
   Registry has refused one in place of the instrument four times, and will refuse it a fifth.

*In witness whereof the Registry sets its hand, twice, as is proper. The second stamp is the one that
counts.*

*Endorsed on the verso, in a later hand:* Copying is permitted as above; the Registry is not to be asked
again. — *F. Oll, Under-Librarian*
