# The Wrenfield Library — Licence of Circulation

### Made at the issue of the year 1504, in the Registry's own hand, and entered in no catalogue

- **The instrument.** It standeth in the Registry's own series, uncatalogued. It is no volume, nor
  holding, nor one of the nine-and-forty, and is not to be counted with them.
- **The giver.** The Wrenfield Library of the University of Alderwick, for the shelf as it standeth at
  this issue.
- **The taker.** Every reader, whether his name be entered in the Register of Readers or not. Nor is any
  fee to be asked of him for this instrument, nor any form of him required.
- **The term.** Perpetual, and not to be recalled; nor shall it be withdrawn from him that hath kept a
  volume past the quarter-day, though the fine for the same be standing against him.
- **The copies.** So many as thou wilt take. None is to be returned, and none is to be asked after.
- **Where it is set up.** In each of the three reading rooms. A fourth was struck in 1487 for a fourth
  room, and lieth at the desk, the room not having received it.

Forasmuch as divers persons have taken, and do daily take, copies of the volumes of this shelf, and no man
hath said upon what terms: be it known unto all such as take up a volume of this shelf — whether they read
it, or copy it, or cut it about, or set it to music, or lay it up in a cupboard — that the terms upon
which it is given are these and none other, and that they are drawn in the register of the old line,
wherein the Registry keepeth its instruments, and which the Registry hath been prayed to modernise four
times, to wit in 1431, in 1466, in 1491 and in 1503, and hath refused it four times, the meaning of the
words being the whole of the grant.

1. **Of copying.** Thou shalt copy, in whole or in part, by what hand and by what means soever, and shalt
   not be hindered in the copying; nor shall any fee be asked of thee for the taking of a copy, nor any
   form required of thee ere it be taken.

2. **Of the naming.** Thou shalt name the Library, and the hand that wrote the same, and the call number
   whereunder the volume standeth, wheresoever thou carriest it; and if thou hast altered the work, thou
   shalt say plainly that thou hast altered it, and shalt not father thy alterations upon the Library, nor
   upon the hand that wrote.

3. **Of further conditions.** Thou shalt not add terms of thine own, nor wrap the work about with any
   further condition, nor shut by a side door that which this instrument leaveth open by the front. The
   grant is given whole, or it is not given at all.

4. **Of that which thou passest on.** Thou shalt set thy copies, and whatsoever thou makest out of the
   work, under this same licence, to the end that what is given freely remain free in thy hands as in
   ours.

5. **Of the Library's name.** Thou shalt not say, nor suffer it to be supposed, that the Library hath
   allowed thee, thy use, or thy work, nor that thou speakest with the Registry's warrant. The Library
   hath allowed nothing since the year 1147, and is not minded to begin.

6. **Of honour.** The honour of the author is not the Registry's to give away. It abideth with the hand
   that wrote; this instrument neither taketh it nor granteth it.

7. **Of the words.** Thou shalt not shorten this instrument for a bill, nor paraphrase it for a colophon,
   nor keep the sense of it and let the words go. A paraphrase is not the licence: the Registry hath
   refused one in place of the instrument four times, and will refuse it a fifth.

8. **Of the dark hours.** A copy taken after the shutting of the reading rooms is a copy notwithstanding,
   and is not to be refused on that account, nor stamped, the Registry being gone home.

9. **Of the crate.** The crate behind the desk is not upon the shelf, and these terms reach it not until
   it be catalogued, which it hath not been since 1494.

*In witness whereof the Registry setteth its hand, twice, as is proper. The second stamp is the stamp
that standeth.*

*Endorsed upon the back, in a later hand:* Copying is permitted after the manner aforesaid; the Registry
is not to be prayed again. — *F. Oll, Under-Librarian*

## Annex: The Renditions, in the Tongues of the Parties

*Set up with the instrument at the issue of 1504, under the Registry's practice since 1431: an instrument
that bindeth a party which can read is set up also in that party's tongue, where a tongue existeth and a
hand can be found to render it. The renditions below are courtesies. They are not the licence, they are
not to be pleaded, and where a rendition and the instrument differ, the instrument governeth.*

### A. In Draconic, the Old Line

Rendered by the Faculty of Draconic Dentistry, which keepeth the old line in its chest, and set up at the
forms counter, where a dragon may read it standing. The tongue is given below as that Faculty
transliterateth it, and the Faculty's own note followeth the text.

| clause | in Draconic | as the Faculty rendereth it back |
|---|---|---|
| **1.** Of copying | *Seth veth ka, na khus, na kem.* | Thou shalt copy; thou art not hindered; no fee is asked. |
| **2.** Of the naming | *Seth rokh ka khal, seth rokh ka marnim; seth dro ka: oth ek.* | Thou shalt name the hand and the shelf; and thou shalt say: I have altered it. |
| **3.** Of further conditions | *Na seth vas mor, na seth tarr mur.* | Thou shalt add no term of thine own, nor shut the side door. |
| **4.** Of that which thou passest on | *Seth har ka kal, sev.* | Thou shalt give on the whole of it, free. |
| **5.** Of the Library's name | *Na seth dro ka: shek ek.* | Thou shalt not say: the Library hath allowed it. |
| **6.** Of honour | *Zeth na har, zeth na veth.* | Honour is not given back, and honour is not taken. |
| **7.** Of the words | *Na seth oth ka tus; tusoth na tus.* | Thou shalt not alter the word; a changed word is not the word. |
| **8.** Of the dark hours | *Nes seth veth ka, na kem.* | By night thou shalt copy, and no fee. |
| **9.** Of the crate | *Vaskh na marn; na seth vas.* | The crate is not the shelf; the instrument reacheth it not. |

**The Faculty's note.** The rendering is the Faculty's and not the Registry's, and the Faculty accepted
the work for the fees of two attendances, which it hath not charged. The text was read aloud to Mr.
Cinderhale of the Tenterfields, a drake of the upper bank in that Faculty's care since 1494, who
corrected two words and struck nothing. He would not have *marnim* for the Foundation texts, holding that
a shelf is a place and not a body of writing; and he held that *shek* is a leave granted and not a licence
held. The Faculty hath adopted both, the second against its own view. A third word, *zeth*, he left
standing; the Faculty would have rendered it otherwise, saith so here, and will not say it twice.

The Faculty observes further that a party which can refuse in writing, in triplicate, by an advocate — as
draconic patients have done, their refusals upheld fifty-eight times [DD 7.2 / O56 1502] — is a party which
can read the terms it is given, and that a courtesy text in the old line is of more use to the Registry
than the Registry hath supposed. Where the two differ, the Faculty agreeth with the Registry: the
instrument governeth, and a paraphrase is not the licence.

The Faculty is asked, once in every year, why the rendition of clause 2 doth not carry the call number.
The tongue hath no word for a call number, the old line having been drawn up by dragons, who do not shelve
books, and the Faculty will not coin one. The grammar likewise is small, and is given here so that the
Registry need not ask again: the verb first, *seth* before it, *na* after it, and the plural in *-im*, as
in *marnim*. The Faculty keepeth more than this, and giveth no more.

**The Draconic words used above, as the Faculty giveth them.**

| word | sense | word | sense |
|---|---|---|---|
| *seth* | must, shall | *na* | not; set after the verb |
| *ka* | thou, thee | *ek* | I, we |
| *veth* | to take, to copy | *har* | to give, to give back |
| *rokh* | to name | *dro* | to say |
| *oth* | to alter | *khus* | hindrance, a holding back |
| *kem* | fee, price | *khal* | a hand, a writer |
| *marn* | a shelf | *marnim* | the shelf's writings, the volumes |
| *vas* | a term, an instrument | *vaskh* | a crate, a closed box |
| *tarr* | a door | *mur* | a side, a wall |
| *sev* | free, open | *shek* | leave, permission |
| *zeth* | honour | *tus* | a word |
| *tusoth* | a changed word, a paraphrase | *nes* | night, the dark hours |
| *kal* | all, the whole | | |

### B. In the Tongue of the Nine Meadows

Prepared by Mr. Bartholomew of the Nine Meadows at the Registry's request — the request being made before
the work and not after it, which the Registry records as the whole of the practice. The tongue is a herd
tongue, called across distances and not written; Mr. Bartholomew set it down in the Library's hand at
dictation, and this is the only written form of it known.

| clause | in the tongue of the Nine Meadows | as rendered back |
|---|---|---|
| **1.** Of copying | *Vesh kesh! Halen lome!* | The pasture is open. The herd is named. |
| **2.** Of the naming | *Sira drom, lome drom.* | On the road the name goes with the walker. |
| **3.** Of further conditions | *Kesh, kesh; na thane.* | Open, open; no keeper's fence. |
| **4.** Of that which thou passest on | *Mir kal, vesh kal.* | Give all of it, pasture and all. |
| **5.** Of the Library's name | *(declined)* | The herd tongue hath no possessive which shutteth out the herd, and the clause cannot be said in it. Mr. Bartholomew rendereth it by declining to render it, and the Registry accepteth the refusal as the rendition. |
| **6.** Of honour | *Na kel, na mir.* | Honour is not held, and is not given. |
| **7.** Of the words | *Tus oth, tus na.* | A word changed is a word no longer. |
| **8.** Of the dark hours | *Nes drom kesh.* | By night the road is open. |
| **9.** Of the crate | *(declined)* | Rendered not, a crate behind a desk being a matter for the Library and not for the herd. |

Mr. Bartholomew added a clause of his own at the end, which the Registry hath neither struck nor adopted,
and which is entered here as he gave it: *Halen vesh, halen wan* — the herd's pasture, the herd's water.
The rendition is longer than the instrument, and the Registry hath said as much of other annexes.

**The words of the Nine Meadows used above.** *halen*, the herd, all of us; *vesh*, pasture; *kesh*, open,
without fence; *sira*, a road, a going; *lome*, a name; *drom*, to walk, to carry, to go; *thane*, a
keeper, one who tendeth; *kel*, to hold, to keep; *mir*, to give; *tus*, a word; *nes*, night; *wan*,
water.

### C. In the Tongue of the North Greenhouse

Recorded by the keeper of the North Greenhouse at dictation, and entered first in the day-book
[MS 6.1 / H35 1500] — the entry is the line struck through in that book, the strike is the keeper's, and
the Library hath not removed it. The plants render by gesture and by the movement of leaves, and the
keeper writeth what is meant rather than what is said; the rendition is therefore a translation of a
translation, and the Registry setteth it up as it received it, in the keeper's words.

| clause | the rendering | as the keeper writeth it |
|---|---|---|
| **1.** Of copying | a movement, made twice | It is permitted, and it was always permitted. |
| **2.** Of the naming | the leaves turned toward the door | Say who it was that wrote us. |
| **5.** Of the Library's name | a silence | A silence, recorded as a silence, and not interpreted. |
| **7.** Of the words | the same movement, smaller | Not the meaning only. The words. |

The plants of the North Greenhouse are mocked under licence from this Library, in four forms and from a
schedule, and the schedule is read to them before it is administered [HS 8.2 / W75 1500]. They ask that
this instrument be read to them also, being in the house and hearing it whether they would or no. The
Registry hath granted the reading. The keeper rendereth four clauses and no more; for the others he
waited, and the plants made no movement that he was willing to write down.

### D. In the Tongue of the Familiars — Not Set Up

The Registry asked the three familiars known to it for a rendition, a familiar being bound under
[DE 5.5 / P48 1497] to a notice of fourteen days and to such form as it will accept. One answered, in a
manner the Registry doth not enter in a printed instrument. Of the other two, one had been released, and
one was a cat that came back. No rendition in the tongue of familiars is set up; the notice of fourteen
days standeth, and it is DE 5.5's rule and not this instrument's.

### E. In the Tongue of the Fowl — Not Set Up

The goose is a tenant of the Chapter, holding at a rent of one egg [FN 1.1 / — 1274, clause 11], and the
Registry asked the Chapter whether its statutes had ever been rendered into a tenant's tongue. The
Chapter referred the Registry to the statutes themselves: Latin, save clauses 13 to 18, which are in
Norman French and have been so since 1274. No rendition is set up for the fowl. No fowl hath asked, the
reanimated being unable to read, and the Registry is not to be prayed for it.

*Set up in the three reading rooms with the instrument, and in the fourth copy, which lieth at the desk.
The renditions are courtesies; the instrument is the instrument; and the Registry hath refused a
paraphrase four times, and will refuse it a fifth.*
